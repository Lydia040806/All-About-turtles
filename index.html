All About Turtles

An Introduction to These Ancient Reptiles

Where They Live

Turtles are reptiles with a hard shell that protects their bodies. They have lived on Earth for over 200 million years and can be found in oceans, rivers, ponds, and on land.

  • Sea turtles spend most of their lives in the ocean.
  • Tortoises live on land and have dome-shaped shells.
  • Turtles lay their eggs on land, usually in sand.
  • Some turtles can live for more than 100 years.
